# Step 4: User Stories & Specifications

<div class="status-badge">Step 4 — Specifications</div>

## User stories & acceptance checks

<div class="story-grid">
  <article class="story-card">
    <p class="direction-label">User Story 1 — Interpreting Execution</p>
    <h2>See design thinking in action</h2>
    <p class="story-text">As a Creative AI internship reviewer, I want to inspect prototypes to see design thinking of the creator in action.</p>
    <p><strong>Acceptance Checks</strong></p>
    <ul class="acceptance-list">
      <li><input type="checkbox" /> <span>Case study section or card includes direct screenshots or evidence of a project or prototype, or a link to where it can be accessed.</span></li>
      <li><input type="checkbox" /> <span>Items or projects show design thinking through the design of the final product.</span></li>
    </ul>
  </article>

  <article class="story-card story-card--credentials">
    <p class="direction-label">User Story 2 — Accessing Professional Materials</p>
    <h2>Find credentials without friction</h2>
    <p class="story-text">As an internship reviewer, I want rapid access to professional credentials so I can evaluate background qualifications and initiate candidate outreach.</p>
    <p><strong>Acceptance Checks</strong></p>
    <ul class="acceptance-list">
      <li><input type="checkbox" /> <span>Resume is easily accessible, and can be download or read on the website.</span></li>
      <li><input type="checkbox" /> <span>Linkedin profile link is reachable easily within the website.</span></li>
    </ul>
  </article>

  <article class="story-card story-card--navigation">
    <p class="direction-label">User Story 3 — Navigating &amp; Comparing Visual Treatments</p>
    <h2>Explore an approachable interface</h2>
    <p class="story-text">As an internship reviewer, I want to navigate an approachable, accessible UI so I can inspect project evidence and/or portfolio items easily.</p>
    <p><strong>Acceptance Checks</strong></p>
    <ul class="acceptance-list">
      <li><input type="checkbox" /> <span>Site has clear navigation structure and makes it easy to find different projects, items and information of the candidate.</span></li>
      <li><input type="checkbox" /> <span>Site is visually appealing, and inviting, as well as intuitive in use.</span></li>
    </ul>
  </article>
</div>
